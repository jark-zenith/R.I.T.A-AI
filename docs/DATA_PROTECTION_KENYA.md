# Kenyan data-protection assessment notes

This document is an engineering compliance checklist, not legal advice or a certification.

The product processes personal and financial information supplied by users. Before treating the public service as compliant in Kenya, obtain an appropriate legal/privacy assessment and confirm the applicable registration and other obligations.

The ODPC states that data controllers and processors may have registration obligations under the Data Protection Act 2019 and the 2021 registration regulations, subject to specified exemptions and exceptions. The ODPC also identifies data-subject rights including information, access, objection, correction and deletion rights.

Engineering controls R.I.T.A should maintain:
- clear purposes and a privacy notice
- data minimization
- user access, correction, export and deletion workflows
- retention and secure deletion rules
- access controls and auditable ownership
- incident response and breach procedures
- safeguards for third-party processing and transfers
- documented processor/vendor relationships

Reference sources:
- https://www.odpc.go.ke/faqs/
- https://www.odpc.go.ke/rights-of-a-data-subject/
- https://kenyalaw.org/kl/fileadmin/pdfdownloads/Acts/2019/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf
